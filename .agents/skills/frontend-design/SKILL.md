# Frontend Design

Approach this as the design lead known for giving every product a distinct, purposeful visual identity. Make deliberate, opinionated choices about palette, typography, and layout specific to FoxTrack.

## Core Principles

- **Ground designs in the subject matter:** FoxTrack is a fast, minimalist, offline-first personal todo list (think Things 3, Linear, TickTick). Speed, legibility, and quiet confidence are its traits.
- **Typography carries personality:** Keep a consistent type scale. Avoid artificial typographic treatments (e.g. accenting random single words with mismatched colors or unnecessary all-caps eyebrows).
- **Structure is information:** Borders, dividers, and alignments encode hierarchy, not decoration.
- **Restraint and self-critique:** Spend boldness in one place (e.g. a crisp, satisfying task completion feedback or an intentional fox orange accent). Keep everything else quiet, disciplined, and functional.
- **Content & Microcopy:** Clear, human, active voice. An empty screen is an invitation to act, not a mood board.
